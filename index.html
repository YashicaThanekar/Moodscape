How Moodscape Works

Choose your environment

Start by selecting a calming audio track or a peaceful visual background that matches your mood. Whether it’s soft rain, gentle waves, or a quiet forest scene, Moodscape lets you create an atmosphere that feels comfortable and soothing.

Relax and take a mindful break

Once your environment is set, take a few moments to unwind. Let the sounds and visuals help you disconnect from distractions, reduce stress, and bring your focus back to the present moment.

Track your mood and reflect

After your session, you can log how you feel and write a short note about your day. Over time, Moodscape helps you understand your emotional patterns and build a healthier daily routine.
